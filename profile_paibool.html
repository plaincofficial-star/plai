Dr.Paibool Kitworawut.

Dr.Paibool holds a degree in Industrial Engineering from King Mongkut's University of Technology North Bangkok (KMUTNB) and is a prestigious German Government Scholarship alumnus. With an extensive professional background in prominent large-scale organizations—spanning retail, building materials, oil refining, and biomass power plants—he brings deep multi-industry expertise to enterprise transformation.Furthermore, Dr. Paibool is a distinguished guest lecturer at several leading universities and has served as a trusted corporate consultant for over 25 years. He has successfully partnered with more than 50 organizations across diverse sectors, specializing in business process improvement, Total Productive Maintenance (TPM), and carbon reduction initiatives. Notably, he is certified as a Supply Chain Management consultant from the Modular Learning System (MLS) of UNCTAD, and he is a leading expert in optimizing biomass power plant operations to maximize efficiency and significantly reduce operational costs.
